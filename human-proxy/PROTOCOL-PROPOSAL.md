## 2. Human-paced interface and counter

- Ordinary keeps its exact existing 150 ms / 18-tick interface. Counter uses a new external wrapper with exactly 250 ms / 30-tick packet age, 30 Hz decisions (every four ticks beginning tick 30), pulse-only throw/recall, held continuous controls, copied canonical sensorPacket fields, and the exact imported motorTransform and noiseSample functions. No controller-internal delay queue and no second noise transform.
- Public perception is the same MODE_B schema: 120-degree cone, unlimited range, no obstacle occlusion. Hidden bodies/spears remain null. No world object, evaluator geometry diagnostics, current sensor packet, event stream, future simulation, reset notifications, or exact opponent policy parameters enter the counter.
- Counter is reactive, not a rollout planner. It tracks visible opponent/spear observations with timestamps, may linearly extrapolate using observed velocities and the public 12-unit spear speed plus known latency, and uses public obstacle rectangles for geometric movement clearance. It clears stale estimates only from observed score changes, never private reset truth. Unseen enemy spear is unknown, not assumed away. A recently visible away spear enables a brief, bounded opportunity estimate; uncertainty expires conservatively.
- Maintain about 5.25 center-to-center units while enemy spear is held/unknown; prefer lateral movement while avoiding the boundary and obstacles. Permit a 4.5-unit spacing target during a visibly supported enemy-spear-away window, without blindly chasing behind an unseen opponent. Choose the geometrically freer lateral direction, with deterministic tie breaking and hysteresis to avoid oscillation.
- Prioritize lateral escape from a visible outbound/returning spear whose latency-adjusted straight trajectory threatens the body; use public speed/geometry, not simulator rollouts. Body movement can change direction instantly and is independent of facing. Do not add a fictitious movement turn delay. Facing turns at 2*pi rad/s, which matters for reacquiring visibility and aiming; throws require the observed facing to be aligned with the desired lead aim.
- Punish while the visible enemy spear is OUTBOUND/EMBEDDED/RETURNING and our spear is held: aim using observed opponent position/velocity and a bounded linear intercept; throw on a legal aligned opportunity. Do not fire indiscriminately while the enemy is held/unknown. Recall our embedded spear promptly for availability; retain public own-command memory so delayed HELD packets cannot cause repeated throws. Log reasons and rejected opportunities only to evaluator output.
- At 4 units, the naive 4/12 - .25 leaves only .083 seconds / .333 units lateral travel, already below body radius .35 even before launch offset, collision timing, decision phase, walls or visibility. A conservative straight-on sufficient clearance bound is D >= launch radius .35 + target radius .35 + 12*(.25 + 1/30 + .35/4) = 5.15 units. The 5.25 spacing target is a small geometric buffer, not a guarantee: moving/aiming opponents, stale velocity, discrete collision resolution, blind approaches and blocked sidesteps can defeat it. Actual collision tests verify representative cases rather than assuming this bound proves avoidability.
- Asymmetry is explicit: 250 ms counter vs 150 ms ordinary is the user-requested primary. A matched-250 ordinary diagnostic is excluded from this initial budget because the ordinary benchmark constructor fixes 150-ms timestamp compensation; a naive wrapper swap would not be an equivalent embodiment. If later necessary, propose a separately reviewed adapter rather than silently altering the ordinary.

## 3. Measurements (truth used by logger only)

Primary: counter net physical HITs/min = (counter HIT count - ordinary HIT count) / exact elapsed minutes, counting both simultaneous hits. Also report both gross HIT rates, outbound/returning counts, win/tie/loss bouts, exposure time and total throws. Reconcile HIT events with final scores exactly. Score resets are not independent samples.

Uncertainty: average the two seat rates within each seed cluster; report mean and deterministic 20,000-resample percentile bootstrap 95% interval over the 32 cluster means. The cluster is the unit of resampling; never resample hits or individual bouts. Report seat-specific rates descriptively as well.

Predeclared mutually exclusive classification, with margin 1 net HIT/min (= five hits in a five-minute bout):
1. Counter beats ordinary if lower 95% bound > 0.
2. Counter neutralizes within margin if not (1), and lower bound >= -1. This is practical noninferiority, not proof of identical strength or defensive shutout.
3. Ordinary still dominates this proxy if upper bound < -1.
4. Otherwise inconclusive. A null finding cannot authorize rule repair or be called dominance.

Distance/lineage: assign each actual THROW a unique episode/owner/sequence ID; preserve it across EMBED and RECALL_START through RETURNING HIT, and terminate on hit/reset/neutralization/return-complete. Record launch-time attacker and defender center positions, true THROW origin, launch center separation, origin-to-defender distance, impact-time attacker/defender center separation from HIT event, launch-origin-to-impact travel displacement, hit phase, projectile age, launch and impact tick. A RETURNING hit retains original throw ID and adds its RECALL_START geometry/time. Report all-hit and phase-split median/IQR/p10/p90 plus bins <2, 2–4, 4–5.25, >=5.25 units for launch and impact center distance separately, both sides. No relabeling impact separation as throw distance. Missing lineage is a failed measurement check, not silently dropped data.

Cornering, both sides: log wall/obstacle proximity separately from geometric confinement. At 30-Hz truth samples evaluate 32 evenly spaced one-unit straight center-displacement segments against radius-expanded obstacles and radius-inset arena. Report free direction fraction and free retreat fraction (directions with dot against opponent-bearing <= -0.5). Geometrically retreat-blocked means no collision-free one-unit retreat direction; pressured cornering additionally requires opponent center distance <= 5.25 and largest contiguous free direction arc <= pi. This operational definition does not claim pathwise escape is impossible. Report time fraction, episodes lasting >=0.5 sec, exit by movement vs hit/reset, and HITs delivered/received while pressured-cornered; near-wall alone never counts. Regular samples are captured at pre-step tick multiples of four and represent the following four simulation ticks (clipped at termination), with the resulting 30-Hz occupancy explicitly approximate. HIT cornering is recomputed from exact pre-reset HIT attacker_pos/victim_pos and frozen geometry, never from the already-reset world. Record representative states for independent geometric audit.

Manipulation checks: actual receipt-minus-sensor age, decisions/pulses, counter distance occupancy (<4, 4–5.25, >=5.25), observed away-window opportunities/attempts/actual throws, threatened-spear observations/dodge choices, and corresponding own/enemy HIT counts. If counter fails these intended behaviors, label test invalid for the intended counterplay claim rather than explaining defeat as a game-rule defect.
