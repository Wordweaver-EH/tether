# Phase 4 evaluation plan (specified before the definitive run)

- Preserve all SPEC constants. Experimental physics overrides are named conditions only; no repair is adopted.
- Freeze source after independent review. Record the SHA-256 of every arena/src module before and after each definitive run; reject changed-source evidence.
- Search on final tuned mind and named competitors: both ES and coevolution, exactly 12 generations each, 45 simulated seconds per training bout, common random seeds fully crossed with both modes and seats, one worker. The optimizer is a bounded heuristic, not a proof of globally optimal play. Historical hall fitness is a search heuristic across evolving opponent sets.
- Search validation: 12 held-out seeds, 300-second bouts, every seed crossed with both modes and seats. This validation may select the highest aggregate win-point candidate among the three returned finalists; it is therefore not the final unbiased estimate of the selected candidate.
- Independent confirmation: fresh seeds starting at 1700431, 16 seeds, 300-second bouts, both modes and seats. Candidates are the selected finalist, directShooter, immediateRecaller, and the untrained anchor seed policy. Rivals are all named opponents, the three search finalists, and the anchor seed policy, excluding self-pairs. Do not retune on confirmation results.
- Primary statistic: win points (win=1, draw=0.5, loss=0), with 95% percentile intervals from 2,000 seed-cluster bootstrap replicates. Retain W/D/L counts and per-mode/per-seat results. Intervals are conditional on the sampled seed distribution and fixed rival/arena collection; identical cluster outcomes can collapse an interval and do not establish certainty about unseen opponents or layouts.
- Mechanism outcomes: outbound/returning hit share, embeds per throw, and reset-safe second-location fraction. Many embeds alone are not evidence of intentional persistent-anchor play.
- Universal/simple-strategy dominance is not asserted from an aggregate win rate. Look for counterexamples, per-opponent results, and uncertainty. Failure to find an anchor strategy is not proof none exists.
- Preliminary physics screens are separate: frozen v1 mind plus deterministic arithmetic, two generations, 20-second training, 30-second validation, two seeds. All six conditions use the same source/config. These low-power screens can suggest candidates, not establish a rule repair. In particular the v1 mind assumes default projectile speeds and is not a condition-aware comparator.
- Preserve superseded diagnostics separately; never pool them with definitive results.
