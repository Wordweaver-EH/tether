# C1/C2 ordinary-experience fixed panel: mixed result

## Bottom line

Ordinary experience produced a working prediction-error control state that carried into the six new encounter conditions and causally changed emitted gaze commands. This panel did **not** establish ordinary episodic retrieval or selected-content route generalization: the opponent remained visible at every delayed decision, so there were zero retrieval opportunities. It established neither forecasting-accuracy nor competitive improvement, and supplies no evidence about subjective experience.

## Execution and integrity

Exactly the predeclared panel ran once: two seeds, four eight-second acquisition episodes, six ten-second intact held-out drivers, and 78 yoked controller evaluations (13 arms for each held-out tape). No cases, seeds, horizons, or source constants were changed after outcomes. All 100 raw artifacts and all zero-opportunity cells are retained.

The frozen validator passed 117 checks with no problems. All six live-driver/intact-replay identities and all six clean-reset restoration identities passed. Every fixed-time reconnect matched its persistent cut before reconnection. The independent audit also checked raw records and forecast arithmetic. Raw replay records are repeated counterfactual evaluations of six fixed sensory tapes, not independent experimental subjects or alternative game trajectories.

## What transferred

Each seed acquired 50 genuine assessed predictions across its two acquisition episodes. Terminal error EWMA was 0.410865 for seed 41041 and 0.402775 for seed 41047. Only that empirical reliability aggregate crossed episode boundaries; beliefs, plans, episodic targets and other learned fields restarted from native defaults.

In every held-out case, disconnecting monitor feedback or monitor-to-control delivery changed actual emitted commands. All differences were aim/gaze; movement, throw and recall outputs were unchanged. Immediate first-divergence checks isolated the monitor/attention path, with the same earlier actions, delayed input, selected packet and planner/memory recipients. Monitor forcing also initiated branch computation, but this does not establish a tactical improvement.

Each held-out row contains 1,200 live ticks. The action-difference counts below compare yoked arms with intact replay; they are descriptive counts, not success rates.

Thus acquired versus pristine reliability changed gaze in four of six cases, but did not improve the frozen forecast error series. Errors and forecast revisions were identical across the monitor comparison arms on these fixed tapes. Later sensory evidence changed forecasts even with monitor paths disconnected; those changes are not evidence that monitor feedback improved the predictor. Reconnecting either monitor path at the prespecified four-second boundary restored intact subsequent gaze outputs in all six cases; this is output restoration, not proof that all internal histories became identical.

## Recovery and missing C1 opportunity

The intact held-out runs contained 188 assessed forecasts and 78 large discrepancies. Sixty-nine of those overlapping large-error opportunities had a later low-error/no-replan state within the episode. The stricter endpoint, where the first post-error velocity-revised forecast itself later had lower error and a low-error/no-replan state, occurred in 17 opportunities and was absent in both reversed-offset cases (0 of 13 each). These are correlated, overlapping within-horizon events, not independent trials or permanent recovery. Both offset-diagonal and reversed-offset cases ended high-error for both seeds; only the closer-staggered cases ended low-error.

All held-out delayed decisions still saw the opponent. Ordinary packets were encoded, but **zero episodic reads occurred**. Memory-write, memory-read, selected-content attention, and planner-delivery cuts produced zero output differences in this panel. The lack of a disappearance opportunity prevents a conclusion that memory failed, or that it generalized. Earlier single-encoding synthetic unit witnesses remain distinct evidence and cannot fill this ordinary-experience gap.

## Boundaries and next decision

The result supports a narrow ordinary-experience prediction-error-to-gaze control effect in this small passive-opponent panel. It does not establish the whole C1/C2 mechanism milestone, broad generalization, competitive advantage, new kinematic learning, calibrated confidence, or consciousness. The native pending-recall-plan invalidation witness remains unverified.

A next proposal may deliberately verify a legal visibility-loss/reacquisition opportunity using sensor-only preflight before any controller outcomes, then freeze one small new protocol. That would be a separate study, not a replacement or favorable rerun of this panel.

The production candidate has a verified public backup. The validation protocol and this attempt are retained locally; the protocol was frozen in two read-only copies before execution.
