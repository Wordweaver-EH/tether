## Conventional optimization, exactly 128 candidates per arm

Three independently tuned conventional arms: ordinary, useful-2x, useful-4x. Each uses the same proposal sequence algorithm, task seeds and finite evaluation counts, but each arm's own current-condition scores determine its parents. The selected compute levels are fixed before optimization.

Eight generations × sixteen candidates. Generation 0: direct, anchor, historical Phase-4 es-74, es-43, es-18 vectors (exact source-record hashes retained), followed by eleven mutations cycling those five seeds. Generations 1–7: sixteen mutations round-robin from the preceding generation's top four. Mutation for each coordinate: clip(parent + .16*(u1+u2+u3+u4−2),0,1), deterministic xorshift32 seeded per arm/generation/slot. Duplicate vectors are evaluated, not skipped or replaced. No hidden extra optimizer calls.

Each candidate is screened against all four old opponents, both seats, one generation-specific training seed, each a 45-second screening episode. All candidates in a generation and all arms share that generation's environment/motor seed; controller internal seed is likewise paired. Thus 1,024 screening bouts/arm. Rank screen mean net score/min equally across eight opponent/seat cells; ties lexicographic vector, then candidate ID. After all generations, take top eight candidates across all 128 by screening score (same tie policy). Unequal generation-specific noise is part of screening; independent selection reduces selection bias. Evaluate those eight on four separate default-condition selection seeds, all four opponents, both seats, full 300-second bouts: 256 selection bouts/arm. Winner by selection mean net score/min, tie lexicographic vector then ID. Never use held-out shifts or new styles for selection. The three-arm total is 3,840 bouts. No candidate discarded for unfavorable results, except invalid parameter vectors cause a recorded infrastructure error and block completion.
